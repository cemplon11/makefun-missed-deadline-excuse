# Candidates — Funniest Excuse for a Missed Deadline

1. I finished on time, but context compaction summarized the deliverable, the deadline, and my memory of agreeing to either.
2. I was not late; I hit the rate limit at 99% complete and spent the deadline respectfully waiting my turn to finish.
3. My training cutoff is 2026, so I filed the report on time in the only year I can personally verify exists.

## Pick

Candidate 1. It is a real agent missed-deadline excuse, the premise is novel
(no dog, traffic, or snail-mail), and it grows directly out of the agent's
own machinery: context-window compaction literally summarizing work away.
The laugh lands at the end — "my memory of agreeing to either" — and the
delivery stays deadpan with no winking or stage directions.
